## From uncertainty to decision-quality evidence

AI decisions in national-security environments depend on more than model performance. Mission purpose, users, authorities, data, infrastructure, security, workflow, governance, and tolerance for error all shape what can become useful.

Decision Terrain brings those conditions into the work from the beginning. We are vendor-agnostic, evidence-first, and based in the Washington metropolitan area, serving organizations across the Fort Meade, Washington, DC, and Northern Virginia corridor.

## What we help teams do

- [**Set AI strategy and readiness priorities**](https://decisionterrain.com/ai-strategy-readiness) — identify credible use cases, assess enabling conditions, and build a defensible path forward.
- [**Test consequential assumptions with rapid prototypes**](https://decisionterrain.com/rapid-ai-prototyping) — build the smallest useful experiment and produce evidence to advance, revise, or stop.
- [**Evaluate AI technologies and vendors independently**](https://decisionterrain.com/ai-technology-evaluation) — compare models, tools, vendors, frameworks, and architectures against explicit mission and technical criteria.
- [**Implement agentic AI in air-gapped environments**](https://decisionterrain.com/air-gapped-agentic-ai) — integrate an open-source agent platform, customer-selected inference, approved tools and data, observability, evaluation, and human control inside the customer boundary.

## How we work

1. **Frame** — define the decision, operating context, constraints, and what success must look like.
2. **Test** — run a bounded experiment that exposes the assumptions and risks that matter most.
3. **Advance** — turn the evidence into a practical next step for the team, technology, and implementation.

The result is a recommendation stakeholders can examine and defend—not a generic AI strategy detached from the mission environment.
